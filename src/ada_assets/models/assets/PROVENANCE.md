Every mesh in this directory, where it came from, what was changed, and its license
(#11). A test checks that this table lists exactly the meshes in the directory.

- **Immediate source.** Each mesh was copied from a Personal Robotics Lab repository at
  the commit shown. All three repositories are BSD-3-Clause.
- **Changes.** In ada_assets commit `6b22800`, meshes were decimated with trimesh's
  quadric decimation (654K → 177K faces in total) for MuJoCo, and the wheelchair was
  later restored to 20,000 faces (`ef5f0ba`). Otherwise geometry is unchanged.
- **Original origin.** Several meshes came into those repositories from a vendor. Where
  the upstream file was found, it is named and its license is copied into `licenses/`.
- "No third-party upstream found" means the file is distributed in a Personal Robotics
  Lab repository under its BSD-3-Clause license, and no vendor or other original was
  identified. It is presumed lab-designed.
- Rows marked **to confirm** have no recorded redistribution terms. A maintainer must
  confirm or replace them before they can be considered cleared to ship.
